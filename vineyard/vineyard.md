**Source:** `Vineyard Dimensions.xlsx`, worksheet `Vineyard`.

An empty source cell is shown as `[blank]`.
Formula results use the values saved in the workbook. Fractions are retained
even where Excel displays whole numbers; calculations are not live in this file.
Numbers retain up to 15 significant digits, removing floating-point display noise.
Excel row numbers and column letters are included to identify the source fields.

### Variety-ratio denominator

The variety ratios in Q use SUM(I8:I40) as their denominator. This excludes the four Carménère rows even though the summary includes Carménère. Consequently, the ratios sum to approximately 101.95%. The saved values are retained rather than recalculated against a different denominator.

### Unlabeled order values

The order section has three values of 50 in its unlabeled K column. K50 multiplies their sum by 5 and returns 750. The workbook does not specify the meaning or unit of these K-column values.
